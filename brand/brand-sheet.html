The mark is drawn for the site's olive backgrounds: navy strip, gold lettering, sage-lime roller with a cream handle, placed directly on the background with no card behind it. On a white or light background use logo-card/alt-navy-sweep.svg (the business-card colouring: navy strip, olive roller). Clear space: keep the height of the roller free on all sides. Minimum width 160px for the two-line mark, 120px for the single-line mark, 46px for the icon. Gold with white text fails contrast; use Ink on Gold. Other files in brand/logo-card and brand/logo-script are earlier explorations. Generated 2026-09-13 from the files in brand/ and the site's colour variables; supersedes earlier sheets.
